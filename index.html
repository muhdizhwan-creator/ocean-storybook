A Beautiful Home

In a lovely ocean named Blue Bay, lived a little sea turtle named Bubbles and his best little friend Finn.

There were colourful coral reefs, beautiful sea plants and lots of different sea animals in Blue Bay. The water was clear and sparkling, making it a wonderful home for all sea creatures. Tall sea plants swayed gently in the current, and tiny fish, crabs and jellyfish played among the bright coral.

Every day, Bubbles and Finn enjoyed swimming together, exploring the coral reefs and playing around their beautiful ocean home.

Bubbles was a gentle, curious young turtle who wanted to know about everything in the reef. Finn was a cheerful little fish who loved to giggle, zoom and play. When Finn zoomed ahead, Bubbles swam along at his own slow pace, and Finn always waited with a big smile. That is what best friends do.

One bright afternoon, something strange floated far away in the water. It was small and shiny, and it did not look like it belonged in Blue Bay. Bubbles and Finn stopped swimming and stared. What could it be?

How Can We Save Blue Bay?

After seeing the plastic pollution in the ocean, Mia decided to take action. She gathered volunteers to help protect Blue Bay.

Mia: "We can all make a difference! Here are four ways we can protect our ocean."

🏖️

1. Organise Beach Clean-Ups

Volunteers can work together to collect plastic bottles, bags and rubbish from beaches before they reach the ocean.

🛍️

2. Reduce Single-Use Plastics

Use reusable shopping bags and water bottles instead of disposable plastic products.

♻️

3. Recycle Properly

Separate recyclable materials and dispose of rubbish responsibly to prevent plastic waste from entering rivers and oceans.

🤖

4. Use Technology

River Interceptor systems collect floating rubbish from rivers, while ocean-cleaning systems help collect certain types of floating plastic.

With everyone's help, Blue Bay can become a cleaner and safer home for marine animals.
